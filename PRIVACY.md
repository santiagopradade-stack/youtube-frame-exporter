# Privacy policy

YouTube Frame Exporter does not collect analytics, telemetry, account details,
or personal information, and it does not operate a developer-controlled server.

The program transfers information to networked systems only when specifically
requested by the person operating it. When the user starts an export, the
provided video URL is sent to YouTube and related delivery services so the
requested video can be downloaded. Those services are governed by their own
privacy policies. The downloaded video is stored temporarily on the user's
computer and deleted after the export finishes or is cancelled. Exported JPG
files remain only in the folder selected by the user.

The application source and release process are hosted by GitHub and are subject
to GitHub's privacy statement.
